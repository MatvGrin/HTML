hover, active, focus-visible, transitions, disabled styles. This page is built as a practical mini project, not as a tiny isolated snippet, so you can open it in the browser and play with the layout.

Theory in simple words

Interactive states are part of the design. Hover helps mouse users, active gives press feedback, focus-visible helps keyboard users, and disabled communicates unavailable actions.

How to study this file: first look at the HTML comments to understand the meaning of the tags. Then open style.css and change spacing, colors, grid sizes, or states. Small changes are the fastest way to learn CSS.
