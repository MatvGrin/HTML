product detail layout, thumbnails, price area, badges. This page is built as a practical mini project, not as a tiny isolated snippet, so you can open it in the browser and play with the layout.

Theory in simple words

A product page combines marketing and practical details. The layout must show imagery, price, options, stock status, and actions in a clear order. CSS Grid helps keep the media and purchase panel aligned.

How to study this file: first look at the HTML comments to understand the meaning of the tags. Then open style.css and change spacing, colors, grid sizes, or states. Small changes are the fastest way to learn CSS.

What to try

Try hovering thumbnails and buttons; they show interactive affordance.
