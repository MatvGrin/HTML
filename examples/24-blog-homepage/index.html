blog layout, category filters, post previews, sidebar. This page is built as a practical mini project, not as a tiny isolated snippet, so you can open it in the browser and play with the layout.

Theory in simple words

A blog homepage is a content discovery page. It needs categories, featured posts, recent posts, and sometimes a sidebar. The CSS should help users scan titles and metadata quickly.

How to study this file: first look at the HTML comments to understand the meaning of the tags. Then open style.css and change spacing, colors, grid sizes, or states. Small changes are the fastest way to learn CSS.

What to try

The cards use different sizes to avoid a flat repeated pattern.
