Theory in simple words

A hero section is the first strong message of a landing page. It usually combines a headline, short value proposition, calls to action, and a visual preview. CSS controls rhythm: spacing, contrast, and responsive stacking.

How to study this file: first look at the HTML comments to understand the meaning of the tags. Then open style.css and change spacing, colors, grid sizes, or states. Small changes are the fastest way to learn CSS.

What to try

The important lesson is hierarchy: headline first, supporting text second, buttons third.
