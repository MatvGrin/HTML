Theory in simple words

Semantic HTML makes the document meaningful, not only visible. A browser, screen reader, search engine, or future developer can understand that the page has a header, navigation, article, aside, figure, and footer. Use semantic tags when the content has a real role. Do not use a div for everything, because a div says nothing about the content.

How to study this file: first look at the HTML comments to understand the meaning of the tags. Then open style.css and change spacing, colors, grid sizes, or states. Small changes are the fastest way to learn CSS.

What to try

Try reading the HTML from top to bottom. Even without CSS, the structure should still make sense.
